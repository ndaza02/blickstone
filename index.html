How We Work

A clear process.
A controlled project.

Every project follows a structured process, so you always know what stage your project is at and what happens next.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    You reach out, and we log your project details and requirements.

  2. 02

    Site Visit & Assessment

    Our team inspects the site and assesses the scope of work. A site visit fee applies, deductible upon project confirmation.

  3. 03

    BOQ & Costing

    Our Quantity Surveyor prepares a professional Bill of Quantities and cost breakdown.

  4. 04

    Proposal & Quotation

    You receive a clear, itemised quotation for the project.

  5. 05

    Contract & Deposit

    We sign a structured agreement and you pay your deposit to begin.

  6. 06

    Construction

    Our team executes the work with quality control and progress inspections at every stage.

  7. 07

    Handover

    Final inspection, snag resolution and formal handover of your completed project.

  8. Start at step 01

    Tell us about your project and we'll take it from there.
