Pricing

We don't publish a price list.
We publish how we price.

Two houses on the same street can differ by thousands, because the ground, the span, the finishes and what is already standing are never the same. A rate card would be a guess dressed up as a number.

So we cost every project from a professional Bill of Quantities, and we are completely open about the part we can publish: what you pay to start, and how the balance is spread.

What is fixed and published

  • Deposit percentages, tiered by project size — from 50% down to 30%
  • Balance spread over 12–24 months, in stages linked to progress
  • Every quotation itemised, so you see what each line costs
  • A signed contract before any work starts

What Moves The Price

Why two similar-looking jobs quote differently.

Worth knowing before you compare quotations — from us or anyone else.

Scope and size

Floor area, number of rooms, spans and storeys. The single biggest driver, and the one most people underestimate.

Ground and site conditions

Rock, poor soil, slope or drainage problems can change foundation costs substantially. This is what a site visit is really for.

Materials and finishes

The same house in different tiles, roofing and fittings is a different price. Your BOQ prices the specification you actually choose.

What is already standing

On an incomplete house, the condition of existing work decides how much can be built on and how much has to be corrected first.

Access and location

How easily materials and people reach the site, and how far it is from Bulawayo. Travel is priced into the quotation, not sprung on you later.

Approvals and documentation

Plans, project registration and the paperwork a compliant build needs. We handle it; the cost of it appears as its own line.

Questions

The things people ask before they commit.

Still unsure about something? — we would rather answer it now than after you have signed.

Why won't you just tell me a price per square metre?

Because a rate per square metre hides everything that actually varies — foundations, finishes, access, what is already built. A number given over the phone is either padded to be safe or too low to hold, and both end badly. The BOQ exists so you get a figure that survives contact with the site.

Is the quotation free?

The quotation itself is, and comes with no obligation. The site visit that makes it possible carries a fee, which is deducted from your project cost when you confirm the project — so if you build with us, you are not out of pocket for it.

Can I supply my own materials?

Often yes, and the BOQ separates labour from materials so you can see exactly what you would be taking on. Raise it at site visit stage and we will price it both ways, so you can compare.

What happens if the scope changes mid-project?

Changes are priced and agreed in writing before the work is done, against the original BOQ. You will not find out about extra cost after the fact — that is the point of working from an itemised document.

Do you price for tenders and public projects?

Yes. We are registered with the Procurement Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe under category SC006 — Construction and Civil Works — so we can be engaged on public procurement work, and we prepare BOQs and tender pricing for other contractors as a service in its own right.

Can I get just the BOQ, without you doing the build?

Yes — quantity surveying and BOQ preparation is a service we sell on its own, priced as a professional fee. Plenty of clients use it to sanity-check quotations they have received elsewhere.
