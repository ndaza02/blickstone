Sites We Already Know

A building site is a store of valuable things in an open field.

Cement, timber, roofing sheets, tools. Most of it is portable and none of it is nailed down until the build is finished. Losses on site do not just cost the replacement — they stop the programme while you wait for it.

Because Sentinel sits inside the same company as our construction division, we understand what is on a site, when it is most exposed, and what should be there at any given stage.

How We Work

The same four steps we use on every assignment.

Security arrangements fail when nobody wrote down what was being covered. This is how we avoid that.

  1. 01

    Understand the need

    What is being protected, from what, and what has already gone wrong before.

  2. 02

    Plan the work

    Scope, hours, people and approach agreed before anyone is posted anywhere.

  3. 03

    Execute professionally

    Discipline, communication and attention to detail. Turning up, on time, every time.

  4. 04

    Review and improve

    We assess how the arrangement is working, address concerns and adjust the cover.
